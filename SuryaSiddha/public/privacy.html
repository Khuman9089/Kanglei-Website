2. Information We Collect & Why

A. Geographic Location (GPS / Coordinates)

To accurately calculate Vedic astronomical timings (Local Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise, Tithi start/end times, Nakshatras, Rahu Kaal, and Choghadiyas), the app requests access to your approximate or precise geographic location.

  • Location data is used locally in real-time to compute planetary Nirayana longitudes.
  • We do not track your continuous background location or store location trails.

B. Astrological Birth Data (Optional for Kundli/Kuthi)

When you generate a natal chart, Manipuri Kuthi, or compatibility match, you may input: Name, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and Place of Birth.

  • This data is used solely to construct your personalized astrological chart and calculate Dasha timelines.

C. Device Identifiers & Advertising

Our application uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements to support free access. Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect pseudonymous advertising IDs (Google Advertising ID / GAID), app interactions, and crash diagnostics to serve non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

D. Payments & Purchases

All digital payments and subscriptions are processed through certified, RBI-licensed payment processors (such as PayU Payments / Google Play In-App Billing). We do not collect or store full credit/debit card numbers or bank passwords on our servers.
